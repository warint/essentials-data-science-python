Essentials of Data Science in Python

Six sessions, from a straight line to modern difference-in-differences.

Thierry Warin, PhD · HEC Montréal

Every dataset in this course is generated locally from a fixed seed, and every one contains a planted answer you can check yourself against — plus a trap: a method most people reach for first, that reports a healthy fit statistic and gets the wrong answer. Finding the trap is the exercise.

The trap A straight line fits at R² = 0.96 and reports a meaningless slope.
